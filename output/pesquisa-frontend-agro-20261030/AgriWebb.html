FAQs

We compiled a list of answers to address the most pressing questions regarding Animal management software.

What is an livestock management software?

Livestock management software is a digital platform that helps ranchers track, manage, and analyze every aspect of their cattle operation in one place. This includes animal records, weights, treatments, breeding events, pasture moves, and cost of production. AgriWebb captures this data in real time from your phone, even without cell service, and turns it into the insights you need to make better culling, breeding, and selling decisions every day.

What is an effective livestock management process?

Effective livestock management comes down to knowing what is happening across your operation at all times. That means tracking individual animal performance, monitoring weight gain and feed efficiency, managing breeding and culling decisions with real data, and keeping complete health records for every animal. AgriWebb brings all of this together in one place so you spend less time collecting data and more time using it to improve your operation.

How can livestock management software improve my ranch?

Livestock management software replaces notebooks, spreadsheets, and disconnected apps with one accurate, real-time record of every animal on your ranch. With AgriWebb, you can track weight gain, forecast when animals will be market-ready, identify underperforming genetics, monitor treatment histories, and understand your true cost of production — all from your phone, at the chute or out on pasture.

How long should I keep livestock records?

Livestock records should generally be kept for a minimum of three to five years, though requirements vary depending on your operation, lender, insurer, and any applicable state or federal regulations. Accurate, long-term records also give you the historical data needed to compare year-over-year performance, identify trends, and make better breeding and culling decisions. AgriWebb stores all your records securely in digital format so they are always accessible whenever you need them.

What are the best practices for tracking livestock on a ranch?

The most effective livestock tracking combines accurate individual identification, consistent data capture at the chute and in the pasture, and regular performance analysis. Use EID tags to identify every animal, record weights and treatments in real time rather than from memory, and review performance data regularly to identify your top and bottom performers. AgriWebb supports all of these practices by capturing records automatically at the chute through integrations with leading EID readers and scale heads, and turning those records into performance reports that help you make better decisions every season.
