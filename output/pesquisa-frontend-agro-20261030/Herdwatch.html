Key features designed for ranchers

Digital calving book

Record calves, sires, dams, and birth weights right from the pasture. Works offline. Build accurate cow families, protect your data, and improve your replacement and culling decisions year after year.

Health records

Log pulls and treatments in seconds and keep complete animal histories. Stay consistent with protocols, track medicine inventory, and catch recurring problems before they cost you performance

Herd management

Manage cows individually or in groups. Record weaning weights, feed purchases, pasture moves, sales, and more. Compatible with Bluetooth EID readers and scale heads so you can process cattle faster and keep cleaner data.

Breeding

Track heats, breeding dates, repeats, and pregnancy results in detail. Tighten your calving window, reduce open cows, and get cows bred back quicker for stronger pounds weaned per exposed female.

Reporting

Generate performance reports in seconds, including calving summaries, breeding results, weight gain trends, and sales history. See exactly which cows are making you money and which ones aren’t.
